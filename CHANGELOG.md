## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-05

### Added

- Semantic YAML/JSON diagram compiler with SVG and Mermaid renderers.
- DiagramC Studio with local editing, AI command preview and history.
- Fidelity and structured SVG import, JSON/SVG/PNG export and protected LAN serving.
