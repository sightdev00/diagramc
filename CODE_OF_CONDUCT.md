# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, constructive and specific.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code and outcomes rather than people.
- Welcome questions and different technical perspectives.
- Respect privacy, consent and the security of other users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and disruptive conduct are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, reject or moderate contributions and discussions that violate this code. Report concerns privately through the repository security/contact channel where available.
